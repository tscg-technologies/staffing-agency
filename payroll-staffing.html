Service Overview

Specialized Sourcing & Execution

Managing the administrative logistics of a sizable contingent workforce can strain internal HR teams. Our payroll and staffing administration services handle the day-to-day clerical workflows—including employee onboarding files, biometric attendance aggregation, timesheet verification, and structured payroll data formatting.

Recruitment Lifecycle

Recruitment Workflow for Payroll & Staffing Administration

Structured stages designed to maintain evaluation quality and timely fulfillment.

1

Documentation Onboarding

Collecting candidate identity, bank details, and statutory forms.

2

Attendance Aggregation

Collecting and reconciling daily attendance and shift logs.

3

Timesheet Verification

Securing client supervisor approvals for billable hours and overtime.

4

Payroll Data Formulation

Calculating gross pay figures and generating structured payroll disbursement sheets.

5

Record Archival

Maintaining auditable employee files and compliance documentation records.

Potential Business Benefits

How engaging this specialized service helps companies optimize operational efficiency.

  • Relieves internal HR and finance departments from repetitive data entry
  • Reduces timesheet disputes through structured client manager sign-offs
  • Ensures organized, auditable documentation records for contingent workers
  • Transparent reporting provides clear workforce expenditure visibility
